# Literature review

Use the paper-reading outputs and their source SHA/page anchors. Form thematic rows for mechanisms, player structure, timing, assumptions, methods, models, results and limitations, but leave `UNKNOWN` until the evidence is reviewed. Separate early online publication dates, volume years, DOI identifiers and journal ranking dates. Record search query/database/date and inclusion rationale when later online retrieval is authorized. No global novelty claims from an incomplete search.

`emr.core.papers.literature_matrix` deliberately outputs only unknown methods/mechanisms and an empty finding list; authors must annotate findings with explicitly adjudicated provenance before citing them as supported. Do not invent external search results.
