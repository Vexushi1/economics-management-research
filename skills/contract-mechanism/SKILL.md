# Phase 4 contract mechanism workflow

- Declare contract form (wholesale, percentage commission, fixed fee, subsidy, risk share, or net-sales commission), settlement timing, accounting conventions and payer/payee.
- Compute **system efficiency** using an independently defined centralized benchmark. Define whether matching decisions or matching total system profit is the coordination criterion; they are not logically interchangeable.
- Evaluate **individual rationality (IR)** against each participant's outside option. A coordinated channel can fail IR and must not be called win–win.
- Evaluate **incentive compatibility (IC)** against explicit deviation options. Enumerated finite deviations are not a proof about every continuous strategy or private type.
- Preserve transfers and budget balance separately; prices, quantities, profits and subsidies must use compatible units.
- Record candidate/unchecked statuses and source anchors. Neither IR nor IC alone establishes a game-theoretic equilibrium or Pareto improvement.
