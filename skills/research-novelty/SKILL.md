# Research novelty assessment

Separate a proposed new mechanism, theoretically proved new result, and model-parameter variation. Compare nearest prior papers before declaring an innovation, and preserve negative/counterexample evidence. An offline-only literature set cannot establish a worldwide research gap; use `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE` by default. Cross-check current online-first publication dates when an approved online search adapter is available. Never convert an unverified PDF extraction to a confident novelty assertion.
